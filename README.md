## 📖 Project Overview
Ta’ala Nehky is a specialized mental health awareness initiative designed to break the silence surrounding psychological distress. The project addresses the deep-seated social stigma that often prevents individuals from seeking help. By delivering purposeful visual content, we aim to bridge the gap between psychological pain and societal empathy. The project will be presented as a comprehensive digital campaign featuring visual storytelling, educational graphics, and a safe space for dialogue, reinforcing the message that seeking support is a sign of inner strength and self-awareness.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives
- **Normalizing Mental Health Discourse:** Encouraging open discussions about emotional well-being comparable to physical health.
- **Promoting Preventive Awareness:** Highlighting the importance of early identification of psychological stressors.
- **Addressing Misconceptions:** Challenging inaccurate societal stereotypes surrounding therapy and mental health conditions.
- **Reinforcing Self-Priority:** Empowering individuals to see mental health as a core driver of productivity and quality of life.

---

## 🔭 Project Scope
- **Visual Campaign:** Designing a series of educational and supportive infographics.
- **Content Strategy:** Developing evidence-based scripts and posts about common mental health challenges as examples.
- **Community Engagement:** Creating a digital framework for safe interaction and sharing stories.
- **Stigma Reduction:** Researching and debunking myths about mental health in our local society.
- **Resource Guide:** Providing a list of recommended steps for professional support.

> **Final Deliverable:** A curated Digital Awareness Portfolio including a set of visual assets (scenes/comments/videos), a content focusing, and a simple guide on mental health normalization.

---

## 🗓️ Project Plan (5 Weeks)

### Week 1 — Research & Foundations
- Identifying target audience demographics and researching common local mental health stigmas.

### Week 2 — Content Development
- Writing the "Core Messages" and scripts for the visual content based on psychological principles.

### Week 3 — Visual Identity & Design
- Creating the visual style (logo, colors, typography) and starting the design of the first batch of scenes/characters/backgronds/voices.

### Week 4 — Media Production & Review
- Finalizing all visual assets and conducting a review to ensure the project is clear and supportive.

### Week 5 — Final Presentation & Launch
- Compiling the project into the final portfolio and presenting the impact strategy.
